Step 1: Activate Your Email

FormSubmit requires you to complete a CAPTCHA and activate your email on the very first submission.

Because your main website uses a background AJAX submission (which hides this CAPTCHA), the activation is getting blocked.

Instructions:
1. Click the button above.
2. Complete the CAPTCHA if prompted.
3. Check your email (including spam) for the activation link and click it.
4. Once activated, your main website's AJAX form will work perfectly!
