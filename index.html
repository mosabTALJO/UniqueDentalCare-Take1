About The Doctor

40+ Years of Excellence in Modern Dentistry

Backed by over 40 years of clinical excellence, Dr. Amjad Kinjawi, DMD, combines master-level expertise in general and cosmetic dentistry with a warm, patient-first approach. He utilizes advanced technology to eliminate dental anxiety and craft beautiful, confident smiles.

Founded by Dr. Kinjawi over two decades ago, Unique Dental Care is a trusted staple of the North Attleborough community. The modern practice blends cutting-edge dental advancements with a welcoming environment for the entire family.
